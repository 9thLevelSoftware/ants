# FOUNDRESS asset manifest

All processed assets under `public/assets/` are derived from **CC0 1.0 / public-domain-dedicated** sources only. Raw downloads live in `assets-src/raw/<source>/` (not shipped). Processing scripts: `tools/assets/` (`fetch_polyhaven.py`, `fetch_ambientcg.sh`, `download_list.py` + `downloads.tsv`, `process_textures.py`, `process_audio.py`, `build_manifest.py`). Totals: 78 texture files (25 materials), 45 audio files, **19.91 MB** in `public/assets` (budget 25 MB; hosting cap 64 MB).

- **OpenGameArt**: every item was checked on its page for a `CC0` licence badge (the one clip from the multi-licence page *Digging Underground* by Almitory was **removed in the licence audit**: the uploader ticked every licence including GPL, with no provenance). Authors are the uploader names shown on OGA; OGA cannot prove the uploader is the original recorder. The grasshopper clip is a trim of freesound.org/s/401939 (CC0, recorded by straget) as stated in its README.
- **archive.org USC Sound Effect Archive ("Red Library", "SSE Library")**: the items carry `licenseurl = creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`; item notes: digitised studio tapes provided by Craig Smith of USC, uploaded by an Internet Archive account. **Caveat (provenance tier B):** this is an uploader/archive assertion of CC0 over legacy studio effect libraries; the underlying rights chain is not documented. Used only where no better CC0 source was found (street/traffic/rain-heavy/crickets/mower/playground/broom/car/thud etc.). Files from these items are marked `provenance` in the `note` column. If the team wants a conservative posture, replace tier-B audio before any commercial release.

## Processing conventions

- Textures: Lanczos resize (1024 for major, 512 for minor; atlases 1024 wide, leaf atlas 1024x512), metadata stripped. Albedo JPEG q82 4:2:0 (atlas q85); normal JPEG q86 4:4:4 (**OpenGL / +Y up**; Poly Haven `nor_gl`, ambientCG `NormalGL`); roughness 8-bit greyscale JPEG q80; atlas opacity = greyscale JPEG q80 (white = opaque). Sources are the 1K JPEG variants (already 1024 px), so no upscaling occurred. Normal quality was lowered from the brief's ~90 to 86 to keep the budget.
- Audio: decoded with ffmpeg, excerpted, high-passed at 35 Hz (ambience beds use extra band-limits where stated), MP3 libmp3lame 96 kbps CBR, 44.1 kHz. Ambience: mono sources stay mono, stereo stay stereo; **loudness-normalised to -24 LUFS integrated** (underground rumble -26) with a safety limiter. SFX: mono, **peak-normalised to -3 dBFS** (loops -22 LUFS), leading silence trimmed, 4 ms fade-in and short fade-out. Loops (`loop: true`) are built as `[tail X-second equal-power crossfade into head] + body`, so the last sample flows into the first; crossfade X = 3 s (beds), 2 s (mowers), 4 s (rumble). MP3 has ~26 ms encoder delay; browsers that honour the LAME gapless tag loop cleanly, otherwise trim/crossfade at the Web Audio level or re-encode to Ogg/AAC.
- **I cannot listen to audio in this environment.** Excerpts were chosen from titles, source descriptions, loudness/centroid analysis and spectrograms; each ambience bed's spectrogram was checked visually, but an ear check of every clip is still advisable.

## Other caveats

- Soil/humus/gravel/sand colours differ in white balance (sand is yellow-brown, gravel is cold grey); grade in the material or splat shader if a unified palette is wanted.
- `rubber_playground` (red) comes from an athletics track scan; `rubber_black` is the tyre/gym-mat stand-in.
- `metal_painted` is flaking grey paint over rust (strong, high-contrast); `metal_galvanised` is corrugated steel (ridges tile vertically).
- Leaf/foliage atlases are not tileable; they are intended for cut-out cards. `leaf_atlas_maple` is 2:1.
