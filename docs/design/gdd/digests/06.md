## 1. Scope

Specifies the environment simulation (calendar, light, moon, climate, temperature, weather, wind, moisture, puddles, floods), the time controls and main loop, the opening/founding sequence, overwintering, colony stages, milestones, journal/history, codex, reproduction (alates, nuptial flight), the Lineage meta-store, queen death/orphaning/game over/Epitaph, difficulty presets and custom rules, autosave rules, plus appendices A-F (state, commands, events, diagnostics, acceptance checks, cross-chapter assumptions).
