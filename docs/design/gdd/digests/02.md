# Digest of GDD chapter 02: Colony simulation (reference species lasius-niger)

Source: docs/design/gdd/02-colony-simulation.md (1104 lines, ~15,700 words). Section refs are the chapter's own. "gd" = game day, "(S)" = species-variable, "J"/"K" = directions sections J/K.

## 1. Scope

Specifies the whole per-colony simulation for Lasius niger: individual ant physiology, brood, queen, food and nutrition ledger, task allocation, perception, pheromones, navigation memory, behaviour state machines, cooperative transport, directives and markers, statistics, diagnostics, a worked example, acceptance checks. Not owned (consumed): locomotion, Tb/speedMult/forageGate/surfaceActivity/rain washing (ch 06), carve grid/chambers/dig plans/flooding (nest), food sources/aphids/prey (ecology), fights (combat), alates/nuptial flight/queen death/overwinter aggregate/opening script (ch 06), possession and action primitives (ch 01).

## 9. Deviations and oddities
