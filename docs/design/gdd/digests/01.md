# Digest of GDD 01 - Modes, possession, controls, camera and action primitives

Source: `docs/design/gdd/01-modes-controls-camera.md` (803 lines, about 14,900 words). Binding for `src/game/**`, `src/input/**`, `src/render/camera/**`, `src/sim/ants/actions/**`, `src/sim/ants/player-control/**`. Constants live in `src/sim/balance/player.ts` (sim-side) or `src/game/config/camera.ts` / `src/input/defaults.ts` (presentation).

## 1. Scope

The chapter specifies the application screen flow, the five play views and their handovers, the possession rules (who, how, release inference, death), player locomotion, the default control scheme and rebinding, the camera rigs for every view, the shared action lifecycle and the full action-primitive catalogue, time-speed rules per view, context prompts, inspect read-outs, state/commands/events/diagnostics, 25 acceptance checks, and cross-chapter assumptions. Unit convention: **L** = ant body length in BU (Lasius worker 1.0, queen 2.0, Atta minim 0.35, Atta major 1.8, Atta queen 2.6, Camponotus major ~1.5). **game s** scale with time speed; **real s/ms** (camera, input, UI) do not.

## 8. Cross-chapter assumptions (Appendix F, near verbatim)

1. **Locomotion** (no separate chapter; walker/player locomotion = this chapter §4, AI speed multipliers GDD 02 §1.4, `tempFactor` = GDD 06 §2.6 `speedMult(Tb)`, grip/slope limits GDD 04 §3, nest walking/clearance GDD 03 §5) provides a pure surface-walker step usable by sim and game-side predictor, ballistic falling and re-attachment, `STUCK_RESCUE` relocation, species cruise speeds (Lasius 6 BU/s at 22-28 C) and `tempFactor(T)` per species.
2. **Colony/allocation** honours `ant.controlled` (never reassigns), exposes the allocation stimulus scale `RESUME_LOCALITY_BONUS` adds to, chooses brood chamber per stage, sets queen's daily egg budget, decides when an AI ant accepts a trophallaxis beg.
