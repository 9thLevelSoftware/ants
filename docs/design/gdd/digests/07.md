# Digest of GDD Chapter 07: Species (docs/design/gdd/07-species.md, 1519 lines, ~23,250 words)

## 1. Scope
Defines the static `SpeciesDef` data model, the four playable species (data, castes, life history, founding inputs, scale, diet, nest template, activity/signals/combat, possession, diagnostics, balance risks) and four economy modules: honeydew ranching, wood nesting, granary, fungus garden. Species data is static, not in `SimState` (saves store only `speciesId`). Most thermal/opening/winter/reproduction/combat numbers are copies of Ch 02/05/06 values, which own them.

Sections: 0 Scope, units, tiers | 1 Roster, build order, comparison table | 2 Shared conventions (calendar, sim-native economy, derived stats, development, lifespan, egg laying/caste destiny, speed) | 3 `SpeciesDef` schema (3.1-3.16, incl. validation) | 4 Climate summary | 5.1-5.4 The four species | 6.1 Honeydew ranching | 6.2 Wood nesting | 6.3 Granary | 6.4 Fungus garden | App A state | B commands | C events | D diagnostics | E 25 acceptance checks | F cross-chapter assumptions and risks R1-R7.

Build/release order: lasius-niger, camponotus-pennsylvanicus (adds wood-nesting), messor-barbarus (adds granary), atta-texana (adds fungus-garden). Each species gated by `SpeciesDef.available`; unreleased show "Not yet available" (Ch 08 §3.7). Data-only future variants: C. herculeanus, M. structor, Acromyrmex.

## 9. Deviations and oddities
Stated deviations: (a) Per-agent weights `w`, `W_eq`, `realEstimate` explicitly REJECTED (sim-native economy; `wildColonySize` codex-only); consistent with K. (b) Seed's ~12 BU/s treated as sprint, Lasius cruise 6 BU/s (matches K). (c) Calendar: chapter uses 0-based `d` with d=0 = 20 March and real-date mapping; Lasius/Camponotus dossiers on that basis, Messor/Atta dossiers counted from 1 March (+1.45); resolved: Messor doy 15-19/15-20, Atta start/flight doy 2-8 (not the dossier numbers). Day numbers are default 28-day-year values scaled by season length; validation 3.16.3 states `[0,28)` literally. (d) Camponotus chamber sizes 5-12 x 3-6 x 2-3 BU vs seed's 8-16 BU; laying windows widened to 2.5+3.0 GD. (e) Messor media lifespan 16 GD (dossier 6-9 unsourced); queen 6-12 yr (shortened). (f) Atta max depth 450 BU (real compressed ~2:1); footprint 150 BU (K); trophic fraction 0.7 (Ch 06 D4 reduction of ~90%); gyne/male costs 24/4.8 FL (mass rule would give 42/11.7). (g) No sun-drying of seeds. (h) HP formula changed from 12 massMg^0.5 to 10 massMg^(2/3) (Ch 05 §4.1). (i) Ch 01 `carryCap` override. (j) Sprint speeds, `lengthBu` in BU, Lasius lifespans per Ch 02.

Internal inconsistencies noticed:
1. 3.16.1 says standard worker mean = 1.0 BU (Camponotus/Messor/Atta media), but Camponotus media mean is 1.1 and Messor media mean 1.05 (m = 1.0 anyway).

6. 3.1 `tier` typed `'T1'|'T3'` while Lasius honeydew fields are T2 (and 3.2 `unlock` for Camponotus is stated as age 2 y + N>=60 whereas signature moment says "colony year 3").

8. Atta entrances min(40, 1+floor(N/40)) each with a crater mound: up to ~38 mounds at N=1500 (render/pile budget).
