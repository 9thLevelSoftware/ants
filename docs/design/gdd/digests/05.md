## 1. Scope

Specifies food sources and their economics, aphid herds, prey, carrion and decay, human food and bait traps; the creature catalogue with attack/telegraph/counterplay framework; the bird event; the creature director; rival colonies (roster, abstract model, fielded agents, nests, territory, encounters, escalation/war/raids, incipient brood raiding, destruction, founding-queen threats); and combat (stats, hit/damage, grapple, specials, alarm, retreat, injuries, possessed-ant verbs, stance and rally modes). Also difficulty levers, ecosystem invariants, threat director, state, commands, events, diagnostics, 24 acceptance checks, cross-chapter assumptions.

## 9. Deviations and oddities

Stated deviations (chapter section 7): rivals 0-4 by custom rule (directions F says 2-4) plus 1-3 incipient conspecifics; spray-alarm kept with biology note; Formica-like predator = `formica-rufa` abstract colony K 400, CP 6; thief ant = European `solenopsis-fugax` (unverified, tagged `[E]`) for temperate, `solenopsis-molesta` for south-us; phorids T3 and off for Lasius by default; brood raiding labelled gameplay mechanic with codex caveat (not documented for L. niger).

Per-agent weight "w": none used (only grid width `w` in `territory`/`danger`). One agent = one ant; rival `N` is abstract population, only a budgeted subset is fielded.

Unit conversions / scale: BU = 4 mm fixed in this chapter (Lasius); creature sizes in mm with BU equivalents; map 4000 BU = 1600 plots; "Lasius cruise 24 mm/s" (= 6 BU/s, consistent with K). Calendar: uses 600 s/gd, 25 s/h, consistent with J; all rates already per gd or per s. Prey nutrient values are "deliberately generous" (load-convention compression).

Calendar anchors: heavily hard-codes a 28-day year with 7-day seasons (herb cycle by day 1-28, r table, winter days 22-28, prey/season multipliers, host decline from day 10, flight window days 9-12, ecosystem soak "28 gd", acceptance 5 "day 28 23:00 to day 1 07:00"). Directions K says seasons length is configurable (5/7/10) and code must read it from config, never hard-code 7; also start season may differ. The chapter does not describe scaling of day-indexed tables with season length.
