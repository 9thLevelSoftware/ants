# Digest of GDD Chapter 04 — World, Habitats, Procedural Generation, Human Activity

Source: `docs/design/gdd/04-world-habitats-worldgen.md` (959 lines, ~19,100 words). Abbreviations: GD = game day (600 s at 1x), BU = body unit, `Q` = nesting suitability, dir-J / dir-K = directions sections J / K.

## 1. Scope

The chapter specifies the procedural locality (one square map of 3-5 zones), the full object catalogue, static world queries, and indirect human activity. It owns `src/worldgen/**`, `src/sim/world/**`, `src/sim/environment/human.ts` and `src/sim/world/dynamic.ts`. It does NOT own temperature, soil moisture, puddle depth, flood inflow (ch06), dig rates/carving (nest chapter), food amounts/renewal (ecology), creature behaviour, rival simulation, or walker physics.

Heightfield cell 4 BU (samples = cells+1); nav cell 16 BU (250 at standard); carve chunk 8 BU (500/side); human grid 250 mm; decor tile 64 BU. Budgets at standard (compact ×0.75, large ×1.25): `PRIM_BUDGET_TOTAL` 16000, `PRIM_BUDGET_PROPS` 7000, `PLANT_MAX` 600 (≤24 prims each), `TREE_MAX` 12, `PRECARVE_CHUNK_MAX` 800, `CLIMB_ROUTE_MAX` 1500, `WORLDGEN_TIME_MS` 5000 browser / 3000 Node. `PROP_MIN_SOLID_BU` 0.75; decal below 0.15 BU thick. `WORLDGEN_MAX_ATTEMPTS` 8. `ZONE_MIN_ISLAND_M2` 0.5. Built zones >70 % of map: shrink 10 % repeatedly.

**Size rules.** `INTERIOR_DEPTH = clamp(0.22 L, 3000, 4500)`; kitchen slice width `clamp(0.5 L, 3000, 6000)`, ≥600 from corners; patio `clamp(0.30 L,2400,6000)` × `clamp(0.15 L,1800,4000)`; path width 900-1500; sidewalk 1500-2500; car park depth `clamp(0.45 L,5000,16000)`; bay 2400×4800; driveway/lane 2500-3500; sand side 2000-4000 (cap 0.25 L); rubber 3000-6000 (cap 0.30 L).

**Surface.** Grip: ≥0.8 -> 180° tilt; 0.5-0.8 -> 120°; <0.5 -> 45°. `WET_GRIP_MULT` 0.6; `GREASE_GRIP_MULT` 0.4. ## 9. Deviations and oddities

Declared deviations: V1 smooth dry metal/glass/ceramic remain climbable (grip 0.7-0.75) vs ecology "slippery steel"; V2 dossier "game week = 3 GD" replaced by ch06's 7-day week while keeping frequencies; V3 human durations stretched (sprinkler 30 s = 0.05 GD, mow ≤240 s, vacuum ≤180 s, play 40-90 s) at real physical motion speeds; V4 sim-side `sward` layer added to visual-only grass; V5 afloat ants drift and take 0.03 health/s instead of surviving submersion; V6 wall cavities are free space between primitive leaves, NOT carve voxels (seed section 4 and dir-D list wall voids as carve-grid cavities); V7 `children` has no voices.

Checks against dir-J/K: no per-agent weight "w" appears. No unit conversions beyond BU_mm. Calendar: doy ranges assume 7-day seasons (autumn 15-21, summer 8-14), but dir-K requires season length read from config (custom 5/7/10) and never hard-coded; chapter hard-codes doy windows (mowing 3-19, watering 1-16, door-open 8-14, petals 2-6, windfall 15-21) and "`dayIndex mod 7`" weekdays with no season-length scaling and no statement of which day is weekday 0. Consistent with K: sizes 3000/4000/5000, BU values, nav 16 BU, 1 GD = 600 s, game hour 25 s. Seed says walk speed ~12 BU/s; chapter uses no ant speeds except water drift 0.5 BU/s and multipliers. Lasius `DIG_FLOOR_BU` 200 vs note "max shaft 175". Map-size L in mm depends on species (larger species get larger physical map).

Internal oddities/contradictions:
- `grassHeightMm` is Uint8Array but rough lawn starts 150-250 and sward range goes to 300 (>255); growth cap stated only for mown (120).
- `shade` stored Uint8 0-255 (0 = full sun) while acceptance check 18 tests `shadeAt` ≥ 0.9 (0..1 scale) and 12.1 `fWarm` uses "sun fraction"; scale conversion unstated. `floodRisk` stored Uint8 though defined 0..1.
- Acceptance 25 expects Messor Q = 0 for every paving cell, but 12.1 vetoes only `fDig=0`, standing water, hole, interior slab, edge (paving joints/bedding sand at 2-15 BU may be diggable).
- Sweeping table lists variants `broom wipe blower` but also a "street broom" schedule (06:00-07:00) with no variant id.
- 0.3 tier map puts water drinking/drowning at T2 but 13.1 static bodies T1; T3 regions yet acceptance 2/5 use Camponotus (no region stated); 4.3 validation targets the played species including T3 species.
