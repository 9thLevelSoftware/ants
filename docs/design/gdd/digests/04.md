Source: `docs/design/gdd/04-world-habitats-worldgen.md` (about 19,560 words, 964 lines). "J" = directions section J, "K" = section K. GD = game day.

## 1. Scope

The chapter specifies the deterministic world generator (`src/worldgen/**`, params to `WorldStatic` plus initial entities), world queries (`src/sim/world/**`), human activity (`src/sim/environment/human.ts`) and dynamic world state (`src/sim/world/dynamic.ts`). It does not own air, surface or soil temperature, soil moisture, puddle depth or flood inflow (ch 06); dig rates or carving (nest ch 03); food amounts (ecology ch 05); creature behaviour; the rival simulation; or walker physics.

## 8. Cross-chapter assumptions (Appendix F)

1. Ch 06 (environment) owns surface temperature (`G`, lag), soil moisture, puddle depth (drowning damage is the shared GDD 03 §11 rule), rain and dew, entrance inflow and weekdays (`isWeekend`, 7-day week); reads this chapter's per-cell data (2.4), `shadeAt`, `catchArea`, `puddleCapMm`; sprinklers and hoses add local `rainRateMmH` as specified here.
2. Ch 06 (opening) shows the live site assessment by re-evaluating the factors of 12.1 at a point; its founding outcome model may weight them differently but uses the same factor definitions.
3. Nest chapter owns dig rates (proposal `DIG_REL`), sand collapse, carve-op masking by material (non-diggable primitives and terrain layers never open), cave-ins from `world.cave-in`, flood inside tunnels, wood moisture over time, per-chunk copy-on-write saving of carve chunks with a `preexisting` flag.
4. Locomotion chapter owns the walker: foot offset <=0.15 BU so gaps >=0.6 BU are enterable; uses `grip` and slope limits of section 3; clearance checks with `PASS_CLEAR_K`; follows climb routes; afloat drift and climbing out (13.3).
5. Seed `f(p)` is extended additively with a `terrainHole` mask that removes the terrain term inside masked cells; terrain renderer drops triangles there.
6. Ecology chapter defines amounts, nutrients, renewal and decay for every anchor kind, the `bait` toxicant, item sizes for `food-drop`, creature spawning inside 8.2 territories.
7. Rival chapter owns the rival roster per region, mapping to suitability profiles, nest templates (<=30 chunks each) and simulation; this chapter only picks sites.
8. Pheromone chapter applies the 15.4 multipliers to cell values in impact areas and the wipe deposition penalty; cells 4 BU.
