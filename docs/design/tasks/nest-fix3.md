# Task nest-fix3 — Ants flee water; nest seams left by the flood and founding work (Opus implement / Opus verify)

## Goal
With flooding on, a viable colony on woodland-edge seed 3 loses its queen and four of seven workers to drowning in autumn; the same world without flooding survives. `weather-fix2` reports that the "flee water" behaviour of GDD 03 §11.3 was never built: ants and the queen relocate only when a whole chamber is ≥ 25 % flooded, so a queen resting in a low spot drowns in a chamber that is 11 % flooded. Build the behaviour, close the nest seams the flood and founding engineers asked for, and remove the multi-second tick spikes of the nest system.

## Read in full
GDD 03 (`docs/design/gdd/03-nest.md`) §4.2 (entrance states), §11 (flooding: inflow, levels, submersion, evacuation, drain chambers, "flee water along D_exit until dry"), §13.2. `docs/design/tasks/weather-fix2.md` and `viab-founding.md` with their results in `docs/STATUS.md` (Nov 16 17:00 entry). `src/sim/nest/flood/**` (public functions: `waterDepthOver`, `isUnderWater`, `entranceOpensBasin`, `floodGeometry`), `src/sim/ants/behaviours/nest/**` (how relocation at `EVACUATE_FLOOD` works today), `src/sim/colony/founding/reopen.ts`.

## Integrator ruling R12 (binding)
GDD 03's drain rate is written per real second while rain is delivered per compressed game hour; `weather-fix2` absorbed the mismatch in a site hydrology factor (`FLOOD_HYDRO_*`). That stands. The behaviour the player must see is the design intent of GDD 06 §5.12 and the site read-out: a site read as flood-safe never loses ants or brood to water in ordinary rain; a flood-prone site floods in a downpour unless the player has dug a drain chamber or the mound rim is raised; when water does come, the colony reacts visibly (ants climb, nurses carry brood up, the queen moves) and losses come from being cut off or overwhelmed, not from standing still in a puddle.

## Work
1. **Reproduce and diagnose** on the shared tree: `node tools/sim/run-ts.mjs tools/sim/life.ts --seed 3 --archetype woodland-edge --until winter --cache test-output/viab/cache` (integrator's run: `test-output/headcheck/we3.log`: `queen:drowning 1, worker:drowning 4`, autumn days 3–4, nest is still the single founding chamber). Before any change report: the weather at the time, the site's flood risk as the read-out showed it, the water level against the chamber's floor span, where each ant stood, and whether a dry voxel was reachable. If a flood-safe site flooded in ordinary rain, that is a flood-model defect: fix it in `src/sim/nest/flood/**`.
2. **Flee water** (GDD 03 §11.3). Any adult standing in water deeper than a small threshold (below the 0.5 BU drowning depth, so she reacts before she drowns) leaves her task and climbs to the nearest dry standing place by the nest's exit distance field, staying inside the nest if a dry place exists there and going out through an open entrance otherwise; she resumes when dry. The queen does the same (with her retinue following). Nurses move brood out of rising water to the highest dry chamber floor in priority order (pupae, larvae, eggs, as the chapter says; follow the chapter if it differs) before the chamber reaches 25 %. Workers on the surface do not walk into a flooded nest unless carrying brood out. Possessed ants are never moved by this rule: the player gets the existing prompt/alert instead. All numbers in the balance tables; pure and deterministic; no per-tick scans of every ant (use the flood module's per-nest state and the ants already listed per nest/chamber).
3. **Mound mask cache** (`src/sim/nest/moundRelax.ts` `maskSignature`): key on breach positions and radii near the grid, not on `carve.breaches.length`; then delete the breach-signature rebuild workaround in `src/sim/nest/flood/erosion.ts` (or wherever it lives). Test: replace one breach by another elsewhere; the cached mask equals a fresh build.
4. **Plugs and reopening.** (a) `plugCount` must not count ghost plug voxels at or above the ground surface; an entrance left with only ghosts returns to `open`. (b) Dig plans with source `founding` used to reopen a plug are validated like player plans (warnings, not refusals), so a deep plug can always be opened; test with another cavity inside the wall minimum. (c) If at hand-over no accepted plan and no reachable plug voxel exists, the entrance must still open within 1 game hour by workers biting from the inside along the shortest line to open air (a real dig, visible, not a stand-in).
5. **Queen alert shared with combat**: export `combatHoldsQueenAlert(ant)` (or equivalent) from combat's alerts and make combat skip clearing the card while the queen is under water; the flood module stops reading combat's blackboard key directly.
6. **Tick spikes.** Life runs under load show single ticks of the `nest` system of 4.5–8.5 s, `rivals-agents` 4–5 s, `environment-effects` 0.3–1.2 s, `checkEntrances` 50–95 ms (gate defect L11). Load inflates them, but a hitch of seconds is not load alone. Instrument (probe scripts under `test-output/nest-fix3/`, not committed code paths) on woodland-edge #3 and kitchen-door #1, find each spike's cause in the nest system and `checkEntrances`, and amortise or cache with persisted state where a per-tick trigger is needed (determinism rule: runtime memos key on every input; save/restore must agree). Target after game start: no nest-system tick above 25 ms measured alone on this machine at the time (state the load), and mean cost not worse. Report the `rivals-agents` and `environment-effects` spikes with cause to the integrator (do not edit rivals).
7. **Sprinkler and hose** wet the ground and feed nest inflow at the GDD 03 §11.1 intensity (0.5) through the existing human-events rate function.
