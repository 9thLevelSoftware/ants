# Task viab-economy — A young colony must be able to live (stabilisation round, Opus)

## Why
With no player input only a handful of 15 colony-life runs end healthy (`docs/design/tasks/gate-1B-sim-report.md` §5, §6, §12 L2, L4, L13, §15). A sandbox whose default colony dies by itself in its first autumn is not playable. Diagnosis from the gate: the colony is handed over at about Summer 5 with 7–16 workers, an empty pool and a plugged entrance; the activation loop (GDD 02 §6.6) never ignites at that size, so each worker makes 0.5–1.5 trips a day with 1–2 scouts; Tier 1 food is thin; autumn follows within 2 days; laying protein is withheld while protein piles up in store (App. E 25 fails).

## Design rulings (integrator; these take precedence over the GDD where they differ — record each deviation in your report)
R1. **Tier 2 ecology goes on now.** Set `ECOLOGY_TIER` to 2 (`src/sim/balance/ecology.ts`): herds and honeydew are the Lasius carbohydrate base the budget counts on. Wire the open seams first: plants read rivals' `RivalAbstract.virtualTend` as the tendedness floor in rival-claimed cells (`claimAt` from `@/sim/rivals`); combat `mayAttack` consults `rivals.isHostile`; `tendHerd.test` (0.47 vs 0.5). Fix what the flip breaks in worldgen populate tests (economy floors were calibrated for Tier 1).
R2. **The hand-over is provisioned.** At the hand-over the first workers are not empty and the colony is not starving: implement the GDD 06 hand-over if it specifies provisioning; otherwise the queen's remaining reserve above her own floor is credited to the colony pool as carbohydrate + protein at the hand-over (she keeps feeding the first brood from her body until foragers return — the real behaviour), and no starvation stage or alert may start within 1.0 gd of the hand-over (`STARVE_ALERT_GRACE_GD` exists).
R3. **Small colonies forage.** For colonies below ~30 workers the outside share has a floor: when stores are under target and the surface is workable, at least max(2, 35 %) of workers are foragers or scouts, with at least 2 scouts until two food sources are known; spontaneous departures at small N are fast enough that a worker makes ≥ 2 trips per game day when food is known within 150 BU. Put the numbers in `src/sim/balance`.
R4. **Laying permission** (L13): fix the rule so App. E 25 with real behaviour passes (`FOUNDRESS_LONG=1 npx vitest run src/sim/testing/gate1b/workedExample.long.test.ts`): protein in store above the brood's need must be released to the queen.
R5. **Autumn for a first-year colony**: brood may enter diapause and the queen may stop laying (as designed), but adult workers must not starve while food exists on the map: check the autumn activity factor, flower/herd season tables and the stock target so a 10–16 worker colony can hold ≥ 1 gd of stores entering Winter. Overwintering itself is another task (`overwinter-repro`, not started): your runs stop at the first tick of Winter 1.
R6. **Honest diagnostics**: "Protein intake: Good at 0.0 of need" (`src/sim/colony/diagnostics.ts` status rule) and "brood is being sacrificed" with no brood must not appear; alerts at the start of every game (`food-c-low`, `food-p-low` during the opening) must not appear (`foundingInProgress(sim, colonyId)` in `src/sim/colony/founding/access.ts`).

## Viability bar (this replaces the gate's "healthy" verdict for acceptance; keep reporting the strict verdict beside it)
A run is **viable** when, with no player input, Naturalist difficulty, default start, at the first tick of Winter 1: the queen is alive, workers ≥ 0.8 × the first workers, no worker died of starvation, no starvation stage worse than lean lasted over 1 gd, and stores hold ≥ 1 gd of adult need. Required: **≥ 2 of 3 seeds viable on each of the five Tier 1 archetypes**, and no archetype at 0 of 3 for the same cause.
