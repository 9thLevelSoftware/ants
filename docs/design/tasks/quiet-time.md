# Task quiet-time — The player is not nagged: the quiet-share target in a whole game (Sonnet implement / Opus verify)

## Why
Invariant I11 (GDD 05 §6, `QUIET_SHARE_MIN` 0.75 / 0.60 / 0.40 for Gentle / Naturalist / Harsh over 3-game-day windows of daytime hours outside founding and winter; soak check GDD 05 Appendix E 25; index entry E-102 and note 16 of `docs/design/gdd/00-index.md`) says most daytime hours pass with no warning or critical alert on screen. Measured in real games it reads **0–0.4, and 0–0.04 on some runs**, because `human-event-warning` alerts are raised 14–55 times per 6 game days (`docs/design/tasks/threats-live-report.md`, `threats-calibrate-report.md` §3; `reviews/threats-calibrate.json`). A warning that stays up for a quarter of a game hour (one game hour is 25 s at 1×) makes that hour "loud", so an alert that lasts as long as a human event does makes every event one or more loud hours. Side effect: the threat director sees no quiet budget and cancels every bird event. The creature side had the same defect and fixed it (a strike's warning now clears 5 s after the strike: `PREDATOR_STRUCK_CLEAR_S`); the human side and the colony's own alerts have not been looked at.

## Read in full
GDD 05 §6 (the director, I11, E25) and GDD 04's human-activity chapter (event kinds, telegraphs, cues, frequency per archetype and per `humanActivity` setting, what the player is told and when); GDD 08 §6 (alerts: levels, toasts, cards, cooldowns, auto-slow); `src/sim/environment/human/README` if present, `runner.ts` (`human-event-warning` raise and clear), `cues.ts`, `schedule.ts`, `rules.ts`; `src/sim/progression/alerts*.ts` and `diagnostics.ts` (how alerts are raised, updated, cleared, cooled down); `src/sim/ecology/creatures/alerts.ts` and `soak.test.ts` (how the creature side measures the quiet share).

## You own
`src/sim/environment/human/**`, the alert bookkeeping in `src/sim/progression/alerts*.ts` (additive; no change to what an alert means), constants in `src/sim/balance/human.ts` and `balance/progression.ts`, a tool `tools/sim/quietRun.ts`. Do not touch `src/sim/ecology/creatures/**`, `src/sim/colony/**`, `src/sim/ants/**`, `src/sim/nest/**` (other engineers); a cause there goes in your report with a reproduction.

## Work
1. **Measure first.** A tool-side observer (no behaviour change) on at least four hand-over snapshots (`test-output/colony-life/round-4/cache/*-any.bin`: suburban-garden, kitchen-door, playground-park, car-park-verge; `--cache-any`), 6 game days each on Naturalist, plus the same four with the colony raised to 60 workers: per alert kind — raised, updated, cleared, seconds on screen at warning and at critical, and which daytime hours each made loud; the quiet share per 3-game-day window. Table in the report. Say for each loud hour which alert caused it.
2. **Human events**: decide from GDD 04 what the player must be told (a telegraph the player can act on: ants in the path of a footstep, a mower, a hose, a car) and for how long. A warning that nothing can be done about, or that lasts the whole event, is noise: the telegraph and its cue stay (sound, tremor, shadow — they are the game), the *alert* is raised only while player-relevant ants are in the predicted impact area and clears when they are out or the impact has passed, with one alert per event (no re-raise per actor step), a cooldown per site, and the journal keeping the record. Human-event frequency at `humanActivity: normal` must match GDD 04's table; if the table's own frequencies cannot meet I11 with correct alert durations, show the arithmetic and report it (the integrator rules).
3. **The colony's own alerts** that the table of step 1 shows as loud-hour makers (flood, entrance, food, hygiene, queen …): an alert that repeats for the same standing cause must be one card that updates, not a new warning each time (the app side collapses repeats already; the sim must not re-raise), and its *level* must fall back when the cause eases. Fix what is bookkeeping; a colony that really is in trouble stays loud — the AI-only colony of E25 is the measure, so report which loud hours are real trouble (those belong to the colony engineer).
4. **E25 in a whole game**: `FOUNDRESS_LONG=1` test or tool run: 28 game days (or hand-over to winter plus a spring start), AI-only colony, Naturalist, human events at normal, on suburban-garden and kitchen-door: every trailing 3-game-day window of daytime hours outside founding and winter has quiet share ≥ 0.60; Gentle ≥ 0.75 and Harsh ≥ 0.40 on one world each; bird events are no longer cancelled for lack of quiet budget (count them: > 0 once the colony has 30 workers).
5. Determinism (save → load mid-event equals the uninterrupted run), typecheck, folder tests of every folder you touched (folder by folder).

## Acceptance criteria
1. The step-1 table before and after.
2. I11 met on the four worlds at Naturalist in the 6-game-day runs and in the whole-game check of step 4, or each failing window named with the alert that caused it and its owner.
3. Human-event counts per game day by kind match GDD 04 at `off` / `low` / `normal` / `high` (table), unchanged by this task unless a deviation is recorded.
4. No player-relevant event loses its telegraph: the existing possessed-ant scenarios (`telegraph.test.ts`, `crush.test.ts`, kitchen tests) pass unchanged.
5. Report `docs/design/tasks/quiet-time-report.md`.
