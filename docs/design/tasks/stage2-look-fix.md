## Defects (in priority order)
1. **Rain streaks are broken near the camera.** At ant height (possession, 20:13 downpour) the streaks are wide, opaque, white columns that cover the picture. Required: rain reads as thin, short, mostly transparent streaks at every camera distance from 1.8 L (crevice) to observer height, day and night; a streak never covers more than about 0.4 % of the screen width; near-camera streaks fade out instead of growing (fade by view depth and clamp projected width in pixels); brightness follows the scene exposure (no pure white at night). At ant scale a raindrop is big: a few large, soft, fast drops with splash rings on the ground are right; a curtain of columns is not. Budget unchanged (≤ 2 draw calls, current triangle count or less).
2. **Moisture overlay is too heavy.** In dry weather it darkens the whole ground; after rain everything is a uniform dark blue. Required: an overlay reads as information drawn on top of a visible world. Dry ground carries almost no tint (alpha → 0 below the lowest band); the ramp matches the legend (pale → blue), with visible contour lines at 25 / 50 / 75 %; maximum fill opacity such that ground texture stays readable at saturation; flooded cells keep their distinct hatch. The temperature overlay must show something in uniform weather: at least a readable tint with the legend value, and contours where gradients exist (shade vs sun, pavement vs soil).
3. **Spider comfort "softened" looks like "off"** at observer distance. Required by GDD 08: three clearly different looks. Softened = fewer visible legs detail / rounder, no hairs, muted colour, slower leg animation; abstract = the neutral marker. Side-by-side screenshot at observer distance and at possession distance.
4. **Creature surfaces look plasticky; the bird's beak passes through props.** Reduce specular / raise roughness and add the subtle albedo variation used on ants (`src/render/ants` shader is the reference for the look, do not import it; mirror the approach). Bird: the landing pose keeps the beak clear of primitives (pick a peck target on clear ground or shorten the strike until clear; this is a render-side pose clamp using the height the renderer already samples; do not change sim behaviour).
5. **Humans.** Legs are plain gradient cylinders, shoes faceted, the broom/tool blob shadow is a hard-edged blue-grey quad. Required: at ant scale a human is a vast, soft, out-of-focus shape: fabric-like matte trousers with a fold normal variation, rounded shoe with a sole edge, soft radial shadow that matches the sun direction and the other blob shadows in the world. Triangle budget unchanged ±10 %.
6. **FoundingOverlay in photo view.** It is hidden by a CSS rule in `src/ui/screens/photo/photo.css` only. Make the overlay not render when `snapshot.view === 'photo'` and remove the `.fo-root` CSS special case (keep the generic `.ui-root` rule).
