# Task threats-calibrate — Predation at the design's rate, growing with the colony (Opus)

## Why
`threats-live` made predators hunt (report `docs/design/tasks/threats-live-report.md`, results `reviews/threats-live.json`). Its fix round then sited the jumping spider and the rove beetle at the colony's food sources so that every family would strike on most worlds, and the loss rate went to **0.40 worker deaths per outside-worker game day on Naturalist against the design's 0.061** (five colonies 69 → 32 workers in 6 game days). Two integrator rulings settle the conflict:

- **R26 — the loss rate comes first.** The design's rate (GDD 02 §1.3 arithmetic: 0.061 per outside-worker game day on Naturalist, band 0.03–0.12) binds; "every hunting family strikes on 3 of 5 worlds within 6 game days" does not. A family that the rate leaves without a strike on a given world in a given week is acceptable; over a game year on a grown colony each family present in the world should be met. As a stop-gap the integrator emptied `COLONY_PAUSE_KINDS` and `COLONY_RANGE_MIN_BU` in `src/sim/balance/creatures.ts` (the review state measured about 0.07) and made one test conditional (`colonySites.test.ts`); you own the proper calibration.
- **R25 — predation grows with the colony.** Colony-range siting of hunting predators is multiplied by a colony-size ramp: 0 up to 10 workers, linear to 1 at 40 workers on Naturalist (Gentle 20 → 60; Harsh 0 → 20). Residents that worldgen placed in their own territories stay and still hunt ants that walk into them. The 40 BU nest exclusion until 5 workers and the bird's 30-worker threshold stay. Ramp values live with the preset parameters in `src/sim/balance/difficulty.ts` (accessor), display rows included.
