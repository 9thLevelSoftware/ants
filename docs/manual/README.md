# FOUNDRESS player manual

FOUNDRESS is a naturalistic ant colony sandbox. You begin as a young queen with nothing but her own body, dig a first chamber, raise the first workers, and then look after a colony: from above, from the height of any one worker, or from inside the soil.

This manual is written in the same calm voice as the in-game field notes. It says what the game does, and where the game simplifies what ants really do.

## Conventions

- **Body length (BU).** Distances in the game are given in body lengths of the standard worker of the species you play: 4 mm for the black garden ant. A tunnel two body lengths wide is about 8 mm.
- **Game day.** One game day is ten real minutes at normal speed and stands for roughly 13 real days. A year is 28 game days, seven to a season. Ants move at real speed; growth, ageing and the seasons run on the compressed calendar.
- **C and P.** Carbohydrate (sugars, honeydew, nectar) and protein (insects, carrion, seeds in part) are the two foods a colony tracks. They are written C and P.
- **Keys.** Key names are the defaults. Every key can be changed in Settings, and the key hints in the game follow the keys you choose.
- **Possess and take control.** To take control of an ant is to possess it. The interface uses both phrases.
- **Colony orders.** The panel opened with the Directives key is titled Colony orders.
- **Field notes.** The codex holds short naturalist notes on the animals, plants, materials and weather of the game. Entries unlock as you observe things; the Interface entries are the short form of this manual.
