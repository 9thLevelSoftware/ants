# FOUNDRESS asset credits

Every texture and nine of the audio files under `public/assets/` are derived from a work released under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal** public-domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 requires no attribution; the credits below are given as a courtesy and for provenance. Those files were resized, excerpted, filtered, loudness-normalised and re-encoded (see `assets-src/MANIFEST.md` for per-file processing); none of the original authors endorse this project. The other 36 audio files are original sounds of this project, rendered by code in this repository, with no third-party recording in them (section Original sounds).

Licence status of the third-party files was independently re-verified on 2026-11-12 against the source pages listed here. Machine-readable inventory: `manifest.json`. Full per-file download URLs and processing: `assets-src/MANIFEST.md`.

Totals: 123 files (78 texture files in 25 materials, 45 audio files): 33 source pages for the textures and nine audio files, plus 36 original sounds.

## Original sounds (36 files)

These audio files are original work of this project: `tools/audio/` renders each one from seeded noise, filters, envelopes and resonators, with no input recording, sample or preset. There is no source page; the rendering code is the source (`tools/audio/sounds/`, registry `tools/audio/sounds/index.mjs`), and rendering twice gives byte-identical files. They replace sounds from the Internet Archive and from second-hand OpenGameArt uploads (see Notes). Beds: `ambience/crickets_night`, `ambience/playground_distant`, `ambience/rain_heavy`, `ambience/street_suburban`, `ambience/traffic_distant`, `ambience/underground_rumble`. One-shots and loops: `sfx/broom_sweep_01`, `sfx/broom_sweep_02`, `sfx/car_pass_01`, `sfx/car_pass_02`, `sfx/cricket_chirp_01`, `sfx/cricket_chirp_02`, `sfx/crunch_03`, `sfx/gravel_grit_02`, `sfx/gravel_grit_03`, `sfx/insect_wing_buzz_01`, `sfx/insect_wing_buzz_02`, `sfx/lawnmower_loop`, `sfx/lawnmower_push_loop`, `sfx/soil_scrape_01`, `sfx/soil_scrape_02`, `sfx/stridulation_grasshopper_01`, `sfx/thud_giant_step_01`, `sfx/thud_giant_step_02`, `sfx/ui_click_01`, `sfx/ui_click_02`, `sfx/ui_click_03`, `sfx/ui_hover_01`, `sfx/ui_switch_01`, `sfx/water_drip_01`, `sfx/water_drip_02`, `sfx/wood_creak_01`, `sfx/wood_creak_02`, `sfx/wood_creak_03`, `sfx/wood_rasp_01`, `sfx/wood_rasp_02`.
